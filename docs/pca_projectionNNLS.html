Non-negative least squares with sum-to-one constraint applied to PCA projection

We here test out non-negative least squares, applied to the individuals we projected onto the PCA space, to see a target individual’s PC coordinates to be expressed as a non-negative, sum-to-one mixture of reference populations. For example “70% Spain + 30% Germany”, or “50% Italy + 30% Germany + 20% England”.

This mirrors an admixture-style decomposition.

In the left graph we can see that the estimates are initially unstable with fewer PCs, but stabilize consistently starting at six or seven PCs.

The right graph shows the Mean Squared Error values. It should only show whether the fit improves/worsens/stays stable rather than being an absolute measure of fit quality across different numPCs choices.
